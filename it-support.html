What's included

Troubleshooting

Slow, crashing, overheating, or just "acting up" — I diagnose the actual cause before touching anything, and tell you what's wrong in plain language.

RAM & storage upgrades

More memory or a swap from a hard drive to an SSD — usually the single biggest speed upgrade an older laptop or PC can get.

WiFi & network setup

Dead spots, dropped connections, new router setup — sorted so it stays working after I leave, not just while I'm standing there.

Printers & smart TVs

Getting devices talking to your network properly, driver and app setup, and troubleshooting the ones that have stopped cooperating.

General hardware maintenance

Dust cleanout, thermal paste, loose connections, failing components — the physical-upkeep side most people skip until something dies.

Plain-language advice

If a repair isn't worth it, I'll say so. Not every old machine needs saving, and I'd rather tell you that upfront than bill for it.
