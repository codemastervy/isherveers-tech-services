Drone aerial photography

Aerial house photography and drone footage for people listing a property or improving an Airbnb — clean, high-quality visuals, flown and edited by a licensed pilot.

What's included

Real estate listings

Aerial stills and a short flythrough of the property and section, sized for the platform your agent or listing site actually needs.

Airbnb & short-stay

Hero shots that show the property, the section, and the surrounding area — the kind of image that gets a listing clicked on.

Edited, not raw

Footage is colour-corrected and cut in DaVinci Resolve before delivery — you get a finished clip, not a folder of raw files to sort through.

Flown within the rules

Licensed drone pilot, flown to New Zealand's Part 101 rules — no flying over people, no guessing on airspace.
