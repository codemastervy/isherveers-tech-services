How a custom print works

1. Tell me the problemA photo, a measurement, or just "I need something that does X" — send it over and I'll tell you honestly whether it's a quick job or needs more design work.
2. I model itDesigned to fit using OpenSCAD or direct mesh work, sized and toleranced for a real push-fit or snap-fit — not just scaled off a guess.
3. Print, test, refineMost custom parts get at least one test print and fit-check before the final one, especially anything that has to clip, screw, or mount onto something existing.
